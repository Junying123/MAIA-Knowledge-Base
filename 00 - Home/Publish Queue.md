## Publishing Rules

**What CAN be published:**
- ✅ Approved product workflows
- ✅ Client onboarding checklists
- ✅ Known limitations and workarounds
- ✅ Release notes
- ✅ PM process guides (non-sensitive)

**What CANNOT be published:**
- ❌ Raw meeting notes
- ❌ Internal debates or decision rationale (use ADRs instead)
- ❌ Client-specific sensitive information
- ❌ Draft or unapproved content

## After Publishing

Once content is published to Lark:
1. Add the `lark_url` to the page's YAML frontmatter
2. Update this table with the Lark URL and mark as "Published"
3. Keep the Markdown file as the **canonical source** — never edit directly in Lark
