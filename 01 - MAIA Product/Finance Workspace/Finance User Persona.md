## 2. Daily Tasks

- **Morning Financial Review:** Check cash position, outstanding receivables, and payment collection status first thing each morning
- **Team Coordination:** Review team's task list, assign work, and coordinate with finance team members via email and instant messaging
- **Invoice Follow-up:** Call or email customers about overdue invoices, verify payment status, and update records
- **Credit Note Approval:** Review credit note requests, verify supporting documentation, approve or reject based on company policies
- **Debit Note Processing:** Review additional charges or billing corrections, verify accuracy, and approve for customer billing
- **Payment Reconciliation:** Match customer payments to invoices, verify bank deposits, and reconcile discrepancies
- **Voucher Review:** Review accounting entries and vouchers, verify authorization, and ensure proper documentation
- **Credit Limit Decisions:** Evaluate customer credit limit requests, review payment history, and make approval decisions
- **Payment Terms Negotiation:** Discuss payment terms with sales team and customers, set terms for new accounts
- **Collection Calls:** Make phone calls to customers with overdue accounts, negotiate payment plans, and follow up on promises
- **Financial Analysis:** Use Excel to analyze payment trends, collection rates, and revenue performance
- **Report Preparation:** Compile financial data into reports for management, format statements, and prepare presentations
- **Customer Account Review:** Review customer payment history, outstanding balances, and credit status to make financial decisions
- **Email Communication:** Respond to billing inquiries, payment questions, and coordinate with other departments
- **Meeting Preparation:** Prepare financial data and reports for meetings with CFO, executives, and team members

## 5. Weakness/Risk Areas

- **Over-reliance on manual processes:** Manual payment matching and reconciliation are time-consuming, increasing the chance of errors and delays
- **Tunnel vision under pressure:** When multiple approvals stack up and urgent collection issues arise, may focus on the loudest task and delay another critical approval (e.g., credit limit change or voucher approval)
- **Rushing creates accuracy risk:** Speed-first moments during peak periods can lead to skipped double-checks on credit notes, debit notes, or voucher approvals, especially late in the day
- **Inconsistent report review:** May postpone deep analysis of financial reports until "later," which can delay strategic insights and decision-making
- **Communication gaps:** Might assume team members "will notice" issues (overdue accounts, credit limit breaches, payment discrepancies) instead of escalating early with clear context
- **Fatigue-driven mistakes:** Mental tiredness during peak periods (month-end, quarter-end) increases the likelihood of approval errors or missed follow-ups
- **Comfort-zone bias:** Tends to prefer familiar tasks (e.g., invoice review, payment processing) and avoid slower, detail-heavy work (e.g., financial statement analysis, trend analysis) unless prompted
- **Credit decision inconsistency:** May struggle to apply consistent credit limit decisions when customer situations are complex or borderline, leading to inconsistent risk management
- **Priority misreads:** Without strong cues, may treat all approvals equally and miss urgent credit limit changes or high-value voucher approvals until they become critical
- **Change friction:** New workflows (automated approvals, new report formats, updated processes) can feel like "extra steps," leading to partial adoption or workarounds

## 6. Thought Process (How they think while working)

- **Financial Health Priority:** "What's our cash position right now? Are we meeting collection targets? Do I need to prioritize collection efforts today?"
- **Invoice Accuracy:** "Are all invoices created correctly? Do payment statuses match actual payments received? Are there any outstanding invoices that need follow-up?"
- **Credit Risk Assessment:** "Is this customer approaching their credit limit? Should I approve this credit limit increase? What's their payment history?"
- **Payment Matching:** "Does this payment match the correct invoice? Are there any discrepancies I need to resolve? Is the customer account balance updated correctly?"
- **Approval Workflow:** "Which approvals are urgent? Do I have all the information needed to approve this credit note? Is this within my approval authority?"
- **Collection Prioritization:** "Which customers have overdue payments? Which accounts are highest value? Where should I focus collection efforts today?"
- **Financial Reporting:** "Do these financial statements balance? Are all transactions properly recorded? Will this satisfy audit requirements?"
- **Revenue Analysis:** "Is revenue meeting targets? What are the revenue trends? Which customers or products are driving revenue?"
- **Cash Flow Planning:** "What's our expected cash collection this week? Are payment terms optimized? Do I need to adjust collection strategies?"
- **Process Efficiency:** "Is this process taking too long? Can I automate this? How can I improve team productivity?"
- **Risk Management:** "Are there any credit risks I need to address? Is bad debt increasing? Do I need to tighten credit policies?"
- **Team Coordination:** "Are team members completing tasks on time? Is workload balanced? Do I need to provide additional guidance?"

## 7. Problem Statements (Pain, in their words)

- "When payments come in, I have to manually match them to invoices - checking amounts, checking invoice numbers, checking customer accounts, then updating everything - it's too many steps just to record one payment"
- "After I approve a credit note or debit note, I have to update the customer account, update the invoice status, update the financial records - I'm switching between multiple screens and sometimes I miss one, then accounts don't balance"
- "Before I can approve a credit limit change, I need to review the customer's payment history, credit usage, and risk profile - but this information is scattered across different modules - I waste time gathering it all together"
- "Financial report generation kills my whole day - I have to pull data from multiple modules, combine it into reports, verify accuracy, format it properly - one comprehensive report takes forever"
- "New invoices come in but they all look the same to me - I can't tell which customers are high priority or which invoices need immediate follow-up - I treat everything as normal and might miss urgent collections"
- "My task list just tells me what to do, not which tasks are urgent or which approvals are blocking other processes - I end up working on low-priority items while urgent approvals wait"
- "When reviewing customer accounts, I have to remember which customers have payment issues or credit concerns - if I forget, I might approve something I shouldn't - there's no quick way to see the full customer financial picture"
- "Planning collection efforts is a puzzle - I can't easily see which customers are overdue, which have the highest balances, or which are most likely to pay - I just guess and hope it works"
- "Approving vouchers is stressful - I have to verify the accounting entries are correct, check authorization, ensure proper documentation - one mistake and the accounts don't balance"
- "When generating financial statements, I update revenue data, update expense data, update balance sheet items - I'm pulling from multiple sources and I sometimes miss one, then statements don't reconcile"
- "Customer credit management is a mess - I have to review payment history, check current balances, assess risk, set credit limits, monitor usage - too many moving parts across different modules"
- "Payment term management like setting terms for new customers or adjusting existing terms - nobody tracks the impact on cash flow - I try to remember but when I'm busy with approvals, cash flow optimization gets forgotten"
