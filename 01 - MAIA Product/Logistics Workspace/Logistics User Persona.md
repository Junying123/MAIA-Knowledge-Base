## 2. Daily Tasks

- **Receiving Goods:** Unload trucks, check quantities and product condition, and confirm against purchase orders using Purchase Receipt
- **Putaway Goods:** Move received goods to correct warehouse locations and update inventory records
- **Inventory Control / Stock Tracking:** Monitor stock levels and track all in-out movements during the day
- **Cycle Counting / Stock Taking:** Physically count selected items or areas to ensure inventory records match physical stock
- **Order Processing:** Review new customer orders, check stock availability, and release them for picking via Sales Orders
- **Picking:** Retrieve items from storage according to pick lists for each order using Pick Lists
- **Packing:** Pack picked items, label cartons, and prepare them for shipment via Packing Lists
- **Delivery Scheduling / Route Planning:** Plan delivery times, assign orders to vehicles, and optimize routes with Delivery Trips
- **Loading:** Load packed goods onto trucks in correct sequence for delivery
- **Dispatch:** Confirm shipments, hand over documents to drivers, and mark orders as shipped using Delivery Notes
- **Returns Processing (Reverse Logistics):** Receive, inspect, and record returned goods from customers or to suppliers via Return Notes
- **Warehouse Housekeeping:** Keep aisles clear, racks organized, and follow safety rules throughout the day

## 4. Strengths

- **Multi-Process Execution:** Can receive goods, pick orders, pack shipments, and dispatch deliveries all in one shift without losing accuracy or efficiency
- **Real-Time Coordination:** Successfully manages receiving trucks while simultaneously ensuring picking operations continue and dispatch schedules are met
- **Warehouse Navigation Expertise:** Knows exact locations of high-velocity items, remembers seasonal storage changes, and plans pick routes that minimize walking distance
- **Inventory Discrepancy Resolution:** Quickly identifies whether stock differences come from receiving errors, putaway mistakes, or picking issues and resolves them immediately
- **Batch & Serial Number Tracking:** Accurately records and traces batch/lot numbers and serial numbers through receiving, storage, picking, and shipping
- **Fragile Item Handling:** Recognizes which products need special packaging, proper stacking orientation, or temperature control without needing to look it up
- **Route Optimization Insight:** Knows which delivery areas can be combined, which routes are most efficient, and how to sequence loading for fastest delivery
- **Damage Assessment:** Can determine if damage occurred during receiving, storage, or handling, enabling proper root cause identification
- **Priority Recognition:** Instinctively knows which customers or orders are urgent based on delivery dates, customer history, or special instructions
- **Process Memory:** Remembers completed tasks, pending items, and task sequences without needing constant reminders or checklists

## 5. Weakness/Risk Areas

- **Over-reliance on memory:** Keeps too much "in head" (putaway locations, what's already picked/packed, housekeeping reminders), which increases the chance of missed updates
- **Tunnel vision under pressure:** When trucks arrive and urgent orders stack up, may focus hard on the loudest task and delay another critical step (e.g., putaway or dispatch confirmation)
- **Rushing creates accuracy risk:** Speed-first moments can lead to skipped double-checks (SKU/qty/batch/serial), especially late in the shift
- **Inconsistent record discipline:** May postpone updating records until "later," which can snowball into confusion for the next task/next shift
- **Communication gaps:** Might assume others "will notice" issues (damage, stockouts, wrong delivery mix) instead of escalating early with clear context
- **Fatigue-driven mistakes:** Physical tiredness during peak periods increases the likelihood of mis-picks, mis-labels, or unsafe shortcuts
- **Comfort-zone bias:** Tends to prefer familiar tasks (e.g., picking/packing) and avoid slower, detail-heavy work (e.g., cycle counts, returns inspection) unless prompted
- **Return judgement inconsistency:** May struggle to apply consistent decisions on resell vs. quarantine vs. dispose when returns are messy or partially damaged
- **Priority misreads:** Without strong cues, may treat all orders equally and miss same-day/priority shipments until they become urgent
- **Change friction:** New workflows (labels, checks, forms) can feel like "extra steps," leading to partial adoption or workarounds

## 6. Thought Process (How they think while working)

- **Receiving Priority:** "Is the truck arriving soon? Do I have space to receive? Should I prioritize receiving over picking urgent orders?"
- **Inventory Accuracy:** "Do the physical quantities match what the records show? If not, where did the discrepancy come from?"
- **Stock Availability:** "When checking orders, is this item actually available in the warehouse right now, not just in the records?"
- **Location Optimization:** "What's the most efficient route through the warehouse for this pick list? How can I minimize walking back and forth?"
- **Order Urgency:** "Which orders need to ship today vs. tomorrow? Which customers are most critical?"
- **Packing Safety:** "Are these items packed securely? Will they survive shipping? Do fragile items need extra protection?"
- **Route Planning:** "Which orders can go on the same delivery route? How do I optimize the driver's route to save time?"
- **Loading Sequence:** "Am I loading items in reverse order of delivery stops so the last stop is loaded first?"
- **Discrepancy Resolution:** "This physical count doesn't match - is it a receiving error, putaway mistake, or picking issue?"
- **Process Handoff:** "Did I properly complete receiving before moving to putaway? Are all records updated before the next person takes over?"
- **Damage Assessment:** "Is this damage from receiving, storage, or handling? Should this go to returns or can it still be used?"
- **Space Management:** "Do I have the right warehouse location for this item's size and storage requirements? Is there space available?"

## 7. Problem Statements (Pain, in their words)

- "When trucks arrive, I have to match goods to purchase orders manually - checking quantities, checking SKUs, checking condition, then entering everything - it's too many steps just to receive one shipment"
- "After I put away goods to locations, updating the warehouse location records is the last thing on my mind - I'm already moving to the next task and sometimes I completely forget"
- "Before I can release orders for picking, I need to verify stock availability - but I can't tell if items are really available or just showing in the records - I waste time checking physical stock"
- "Cycle counting kills my whole day - I count the physical items, then I have to find each one in the records, compare numbers, figure out why they don't match, fix the discrepancies - one count takes forever"
- "New orders come in but they all look the same to me - I can't tell which customer is urgent or which order needs to ship today - I treat everything as normal and might miss rush orders"
- "My pick list just tells me what to pick, not where things are or the best way to walk through the warehouse - I end up zigzagging all over, going back to the same aisles multiple times"
- "When packing, I have to remember which items are fragile or need special handling - if I forget, I might pack something wrong and it breaks in transit - there's no quick way to check"
- "Planning which orders go on which delivery route is a puzzle - I can't easily see which customers are in the same area or which routes are already full - I just guess and hope it works"
- "Loading trucks is stressful - I have to remember which orders are on which delivery route so I load them in the right sequence - one mistake and the driver has to dig through the truck"
- "When dispatching, I update the delivery note, update the order status, update the shipment record - I'm switching between three different screens and I sometimes miss one, then orders show as 'not shipped'"
- "Customer returns are a mess - I have to receive it back, inspect condition, decide if it's resellable, update inventory, create return notes, contact customer service - too many moving parts"
- "Housekeeping like keeping aisles clear or organizing racks - nobody tracks this, it's just expected - I try to remember but when I'm busy with orders, housekeeping gets forgotten"
