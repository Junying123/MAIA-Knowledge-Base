# Management Role - Features Used Across System

**Last Updated:** December 2025
**Role:** General Manager / Operations Director
**Focus:** Strategic oversight across Sales, Finance, and Logistics workspaces

---

## Overview

The Management role requires access to strategic, high-level views across all workspaces for business performance monitoring, strategic decision-making, and cross-functional coordination. Management focuses on consolidated dashboards, reports, exception management, and key metrics rather than detailed operational tasks.

---

## Executive & Consolidated Views

### Cross-Workspace Dashboards
- **Sales Dashboard** (`/sales`) - Sales performance, pipeline health, revenue metrics
- **Finance Dashboard** (`/finance`) - Cash flow, receivables, financial health
- **Logistics Dashboard** (`/logistics`) - Fulfillment rates, inventory levels, operational metrics
- **Executive Dashboard** (if available) - Consolidated view across all workspaces

### Daily Briefings
- **Sales Daily Digest** (`/sales/daily-digest`) - Overnight sales activities and metrics
- **Finance Daily Digest** (`/finance/daily-digest`) - Financial activities and cash flow updates
- **Logistics Daily Digest** (`/logistics/daily-digest`) - Warehouse operations and fulfillment status

---

## Sales Workspace Features

### Strategic Sales Monitoring
- **Dashboard** - Sales performance, pipeline status, revenue trends
- **Daily Digest** - Sales activities summary and key metrics
- **Sales Orders** - High-value orders, order status, fulfillment tracking
- **Quotations** - Pipeline value, conversion rates, opportunity tracking
- **Customers** - Customer portfolio, relationship status, account overview

### Sales Performance Analysis
- **Revenue Reports** - Sales revenue trends, target achievement, growth metrics
- **Pipeline Reports** - Sales pipeline health, conversion rates, opportunity analysis
- **Customer Reports** - Customer performance, retention, lifetime value

---

## Finance Workspace Features

### Financial Health Monitoring
- **Dashboard** - Cash position, receivables, payment collection status
- **Daily Digest** - Financial activities, payment updates, cash flow changes
- **Financial Statements** - Profit & Loss, Balance Sheet, Cash Flow Statement
- **Revenue Report** - Revenue performance, trends, target comparison
- **Accounts Receivable Aging** - Outstanding receivables, collection priorities
- **Payment Collection Report** - Collection performance, DSO trends

### Financial Decision Support
- **Credit Limits** - High-value credit limit approvals, credit risk assessment
- **Payment Terms** - Payment term policies, cash flow impact analysis
- **Debtors** - Accounts receivable overview, customer payment status
- **Creditors** - Accounts payable overview, supplier payment status
- **General Ledger** - Financial transaction overview, account balances

### Billing & Payments Overview
- **Invoices** - Invoice status, outstanding invoices, billing performance
- **Credit Notes** - Credit note approvals (high-value), credit trends
- **Debit Notes** - Additional billing overview, revenue adjustments
- **Receipts** - Payment collection overview, cash flow tracking

---

## Logistics Workspace Features

### Operational Performance Monitoring
- **Dashboard** - Warehouse operations, fulfillment rates, inventory levels
- **Daily Digest** - Overnight operations, fulfillment status, inventory changes
- **Stock Ledger** - Inventory movements, stock levels, inventory valuation
- **Stock Recon** - Inventory accuracy, reconciliation status, variance analysis

### Fulfillment & Delivery Tracking
- **Sales Orders** - Order fulfillment status, delivery tracking
- **Delivery Notes** - Delivery performance, on-time delivery rates
- **Delivery Trips** - Route optimization, delivery efficiency
- **Shipments** - Shipping status, carrier performance

### Inventory Management Overview
- **Items** - Product catalog overview, inventory levels
- **Warehouses** - Multi-location inventory, stock distribution
- **Stock Entry** - Inventory adjustments, stock movements overview

---

## Cross-Functional Features

### Exception Management
- **Customer Issues** (Sales) - High-priority customer complaints, escalations
- **Customer Issues** (Finance) - Billing disputes, payment issues
- **Customer Issues** (Logistics) - Delivery problems, fulfillment issues
- **Issues** (All Workspaces) - Critical operational issues requiring executive attention

### Strategic Reporting
- **Revenue Reports** - Cross-functional revenue analysis
- **Financial Statements** - Comprehensive financial reporting
- **Performance Dashboards** - Consolidated KPIs across departments
- **Exception Reports** - Critical issues and escalations

### Approval Workflows
- **High-Value Credit Limits** - Strategic credit decisions
- **Payment Terms** - Strategic payment term approvals
- **Credit Notes** (High-value) - Executive approval for significant credits
- **Debit Notes** (High-value) - Executive approval for significant adjustments

## Key Metrics & KPIs Accessed

### Sales Metrics
- Revenue growth rate
- Sales pipeline value
- Conversion rates
- Customer acquisition rate
- Customer retention rate

### Financial Metrics
- Cash flow position
- DSO (Days Sales Outstanding)
- Accounts receivable aging
- Payment collection rate

- Revenue vs. targets

### Logistics Metrics
- Order fulfillment rate
- On-time delivery rate
- Inventory turnover rate
- Stockout rate
- Warehouse efficiency
- Inventory accuracy

### Cross-Functional Metrics
- Department performance targets
- Cross-department SLA compliance
- Exception resolution time
- Customer satisfaction scores
- Operational cost efficiency

---

## Features NOT Typically Used (Operational Detail)

Management role typically does NOT use:
- Detailed task management (My Tasks - delegated to department heads)
- Individual transaction processing (receipts, invoices, pick lists)
- Day-to-day operational workflows (picking, packing, receiving)
- Detailed customer service interactions
- Low-level inventory adjustments
- Routine payment processing
- Detailed voucher entries

---

## Summary

**Total Features Used:** ~40-50 modules across all workspaces

**Primary Focus:**
- **Dashboards** (3 workspaces)
- **Daily Digests** (3 workspaces)
- **Reports** (10+ report types)
- **Exception Management** (Issues across all workspaces)
- **Strategic Approvals** (Credit limits, payment terms, high-value transactions)
- **Performance Monitoring** (KPIs and metrics across departments)

**Access Pattern:**
- Strategic oversight and monitoring
- Exception-based management
- High-level performance review
- Cross-functional coordination
- Executive decision-making support
