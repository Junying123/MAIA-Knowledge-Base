Some modules appear in multiple workspaces with different perspectives:
- **Dashboard** - Available in all 3 workspaces (Sales, Finance, Logistics)
- **My Tasks** - Available in all 3 workspaces
- **Daily Digest** - Available in all 3 workspaces
- **Sales Orders** - Available in Sales, Finance, and Logistics (different views)
- **Invoices** - Available in Sales, Finance, and Logistics (different views)
- **Credit Notes** - Available in Sales and Finance

- **Vouchers** - Available in Sales and Finance
