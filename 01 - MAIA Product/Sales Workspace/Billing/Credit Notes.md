# Credit Notes Module

**Location:** Sales → Credit Notes

**Purpose:** Issue credits to customers for returns or adjustments.

## Key Features

- Create from invoice or standalone
- Full or partial credits
- Apply to invoices or issue refunds
- ⚠️ **Limitation:** Only one credit note per invoice
