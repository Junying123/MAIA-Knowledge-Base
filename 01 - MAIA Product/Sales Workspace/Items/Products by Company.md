# Products by Company - System Exploration

**Base URL:** `https://maia-oms-dev.vercel.app`
**Form URL:** `/sales/orders/new`

## Overview

This document catalogs all available products (SKUs) for each company in the system. Products are accessed through the "Items" section of the Sales Order creation form, where selecting a company filters the available SKUs.

> **Note:** Products are company-specific. When a company is selected in the "Biller Information" section, only products associated with that company are available in the SKU dropdown.
