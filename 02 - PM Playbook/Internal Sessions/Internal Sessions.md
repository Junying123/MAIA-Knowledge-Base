# Internal Sessions

Internal-only material for the MAIA PM team: briefings from engineering leads, craft talks, lunch-and-learns, and **raw or cleaned transcripts** from those sessions.

## What belongs here

| Put it here | Examples |
|-------------|----------|
| **This folder** (`Internal Sessions`) | Tech lead briefing on how PMs brief dev; internal Q&A transcripts; PM ↔ engineering alignment sessions |
| **Not here** | Client-facing training decks, slide outlines, or facilitator notes → [[01 - MAIA Product/Client Training/MAIA User Training - Slide Content Proposal]] |
| **Not here** | Client-specific training or demos → `03 - Clients/[Client]/Meetings/` (or that client’s folders) |

## Adding a new note

1. Create a new `.md` file in this folder (or a subfolder if you later split by year, e.g. `2026/`).
2. Use required YAML frontmatter (`owner`, `status`, `last_reviewed`; add `meeting_date` when useful).
3. Prefer a clear title and date in the filename, e.g. `Tech lead — PM dev briefing — 2026-02 — Transcript.md`.
4. For structure, start from `[[02 - PM Playbook/Templates/[Template] Meeting Notes]]` or paste the transcript under headings (summary first, full transcript below).
5. Link related process pages in **See Also** (e.g. dev handover).
