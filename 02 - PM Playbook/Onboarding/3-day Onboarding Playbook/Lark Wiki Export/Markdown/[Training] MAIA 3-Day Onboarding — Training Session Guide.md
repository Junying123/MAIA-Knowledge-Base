**\[Training\] MAIA 3-Day Onboarding --- Training Session Guide**

**For: Onboarding Team (Sales, Product, Tech)**

**Session goal:** By the end of this training, every team member can execute their role in the 3-day onboarding model, knows which gates they own, and can use AI tools to complete their steps in the target time.

**Format:** 2-hour working session. Blend of walkthrough + hands-on practice with real or mock client data.

**Session Agenda**

**Block 1 --- Why We\'re Changing (15 min)**

**The old problem**

The current onboarding takes 7+ elapsed days but only \~2.5--3 mandays of real work. The rest is idle time: waiting on client data, sequential handoffs, and scope that creeps in at deployment time.

**The new model**

Three parallel streams run simultaneously for 3 days:

**Stream A (Requirements):** Product finalises the client narrative, tech brief, and deployment plan

**Stream B (Data):** Tech validates, transforms, and prepares client data for import

**Stream C (Infrastructure):** Tech provisions the instance and configures settings + integrations

All three streams converge on Day 3 for UAT and go-live.

**What makes this possible**

**Prerequisites are gates, not suggestions.** The 3-day clock does not start until client data is in hand, SOW is signed, and integrations are confirmed.

**AI compresses the document work.** Questionnaire, narrative, tech brief, SOW --- each generated by AI from upstream inputs. Human reviews, not writes.

**Templates replace creative writing.** The tech brief is a structured fill-in form. Data ingestion uses standard Excel templates. Config follows a playbook.

**What this requires from each person**

Sales: deliver the final deal briefing (signed SOW + final narrative) before handoff. No final brief = no questionnaire = no meeting.

Product: enforce the prerequisites gate. Schedule Meeting 1 only when the checklist is met.

Tech: run Streams B and C in parallel with Stream A from Day 1.

**Block 2 --- Swimlane Walkthrough (30 min)**

Walk through \[3Day_Onboarding_Swimlane.md\](3Day_Onboarding_Swimlane.md) as a team.

**Key points to call out during walkthrough:**

**WABA setup is triggered immediately after deal closure (post-SOW signing).** It takes 3�10 days and has zero dependency on the questionnaire.

**Meeting 1 is confirmation, not discovery.** The questionnaire and sample data are prerequisites. If the client hasn\'t filled the questionnaire, the meeting is rescheduled. Not delayed --- rescheduled with a clear explanation: \"We need the inputs to make this meeting productive.\"

**Three streams run in parallel on Day 1.** Product is not idle while Tech provisions. Tech is not idle while Product runs the meeting debrief. Both happen simultaneously.

**AI generates the first draft at every document step.** Product opens Claude, pastes the final sales narrative from the signed deal brief, and gets a questionnaire draft. After Meeting 1, product pastes meeting notes and gets a narrative draft. Tech lead pastes the confirmed narrative and gets a pre-filled tech brief. Each step takes minutes, not hours.

**Day 3 is non-negotiable.** UAT is scheduled at SOW sign-off. Client commits to a date. \"We\'ll figure it out\" is not a date.

**Block 3 --- AI Tool Practice (30 min)**

Each person practices the AI steps they own. Use the mock client brief in the appendix, or a real recent client if available.

**Step 3A --- Questionnaire Generation (Product)**

**Tool:** Claude (claude.ai or Claude Code)

**Time target:** 15 minutes from narrative to reviewed questionnaire draft

**Practice exercise:**

Open \[Doc5_AI_Prompt_Templates.md\](Doc5_AI_Prompt_Templates.md) --- find the questionnaire generation prompt

Open the mock sales narrative (Appendix A of this doc)

Run the prompt. Review the output against \[Doc1_Requirements_Questionnaire.md\](Doc1_Requirements_Questionnaire.md)

Identify 2--3 things you would edit before sending to the client

**What good looks like:** The AI output is 70--80% usable as-is. You\'re editing for accuracy and client-specific context, not rewriting.

**Step 3B --- Post-Meeting Narrative (Product)**

**Tool:** Claude

**Time target:** 45 minutes from raw notes to reviewed narrative

**Practice exercise:**

Open the mock meeting notes (Appendix B)

Run the narrative generation prompt from Doc5

Review against the MAIA narrative style (Arrow/Thermac format)

Mark any facts that need verification before sending to tech lead

**What good looks like:** The narrative captures the client\'s workflow, exceptions, and key decisions. Anything the AI hallucinated or misread should be obvious on review.

**Step 3C --- Tech Brief Population (Tech Lead)**

**Tool:** Claude

**Time target:** 20 minutes from confirmed narrative to reviewed tech brief draft

**Practice exercise:**

Open \[Doc8_Tech_Brief_Template.md\](Doc8_Tech_Brief_Template.md)

Run the tech brief population prompt from Doc5 with the mock narrative

Check each section for accuracy --- especially integrations, roles, and custom requirements

Identify the gaps the AI left blank that you\'d need to ask product about

**What good looks like:** 60--70% of the tech brief is pre-filled. You\'re confirming and filling gaps, not building from scratch.

**Step 3D --- Data Validation (Tech)**

**Tool:** Script / Claude

**Time target:** 0.25 day to validate full client data package

**Practice exercise:**

Open the mock client data files (Appendix C --- customer list, item list, pricelist)

Check each file against the MAIA import schema requirements (column headers, required fields, data types)

Generate a validation report: list of issues found, what the client needs to fix

Draft the message to the client requesting fixes

**What good looks like:** The validation report is specific --- not \"data is missing\" but \"Row 14 in customer list: phone number format invalid, should be +60XXXXXXX\".

**Block 4 --- Role Breakouts (25 min)**

Split into three groups. Each group works through the new process from their role\'s perspective using the mock client scenario.

**Sales Breakout**

**Focus:** What a good sales handoff looks like

Work through:

What information does the sales narrative need to contain for Product to generate a useful questionnaire?

Write a 5-point checklist of what you\'ll verify is in the narrative before handing off

Role-play: Sales briefs Product on a new client. Product asks clarifying questions. Identify what was missing.

**Output:** A personal checklist each salesperson uses before every narrative handoff.

**Product Breakout**

**Focus:** Running the pre-onboarding phase and owning the gates

Work through:

Practice the prerequisite gate check: given the mock client\'s submitted questionnaire (Appendix D --- intentionally 60% complete), what do you do?

Draft the message to the client explaining what\'s missing and what happens if they don\'t provide it

Walk through Meeting 1 agenda (Doc2) --- identify which questions become unnecessary if the questionnaire was well-filled

**Output:** Confidence in holding the gate. Script for common client pushback (\"Can we just start and fill in details later?\").

**Tech Breakout**

**Focus:** Running Streams B and C in parallel from Day 1

Work through:

Given the mock tech brief (partially filled), identify what you can start immediately on Day 1 vs what needs product confirmation

Walk through the instance provisioning checklist --- what\'s the exact sequence of steps?

Practice the data transformation exercise: map the mock client Excel to the MAIA import template, identify the non-obvious mappings

**Output:** Day 1 personal task list. Clear understanding of what blocks you vs what you can proceed without.

**Block 5 --- Gate Enforcement (20 min)**

**The gates we enforce (non-negotiable)**

**Gate 1: Before generating the questionnaire**

Final deal briefing exists: signed SOW + final sales narrative (\>=1 page covering business background, current pain points, key workflows, integrations in use, scale/volume)

**Gate 2: Before scheduling Meeting 1**

Questionnaire returned ≥80% complete

Sample data submitted (customer list, item list, pricelist, 3 sample transaction documents)

Invoice paid (or confirmed payment terms agreed)

**Gate 3: Before Day 1 of 3-day onboarding**

SOW signed

All items in the pre-onboarding gate checklist confirmed

WABA status resolved (active, or explicitly deferred with client sign-off)

LLM API keys received

Integration access confirmed or scoped out

**Gate 4: Before go-live**

All go-live criteria in Doc8 (Section 8) checked

Client has attended UAT and confirmed sign-off in writing

**How to handle gate failures**

**Client hasn\'t returned the questionnaire after 5 business days:**

  -----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  \"We\'ve reserved onboarding time for you starting \[date\]. To proceed, we need the questionnaire and sample data by \[date\]. Without these inputs, we can\'t prepare a productive meeting --- and the session would be discovery rather than confirmation, which adds time for both sides. If we don\'t receive them by \[date\], we\'ll need to reschedule your slot.\"

  -----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Do not soften this. The gate exists because skipping it causes worse outcomes for the client.

**Client wants to add scope after SOW sign-off:**

  -----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  \"That\'s a great idea and worth doing --- let\'s add it to the post-go-live roadmap. The current onboarding is scoped to what\'s in the SOW. We\'ll get you live on the base system first, then we can scope and schedule the enhancement.\"

  -----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Do not absorb scope creep into the live onboarding. It will delay everyone and deliver worse results.

**WABA still pending at Day 3:**

  ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Default: deploy MAIA without WhatsApp. Connect WABA as a 1-hour add-on when it\'s ready. Never block go-live for WABA.

  ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Explain this to the client at SOW sign-off so it\'s not a surprise: \"If WABA isn\'t ready by Day 3, we\'ll go live on all other channels and connect WhatsApp as soon as it\'s verified.\"

**Common pushback and how to respond**

  -------------------------------------------------------------- ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Pushback                                                       Response

  \"Can we just start and fill in the details as we go?\"        \"We\'ve tried that --- it extends the timeline and results in rework. The prerequisites exist so we can move faster, not slower. Two days of prep saves you a week of back-and-forth.\"

  \"We\'re a special case, our data is complicated\"             \"Understood. That\'s exactly why we validate the data before deployment, not during. Share what you have and we\'ll identify the issues early.\"

  \"Our IT team isn\'t available for the integration meeting\"   \"Let\'s schedule it for when they are. Integration gaps discovered at deployment are expensive to fix. 30 minutes now saves days later.\"

  \"Can you just copy what you did for \[other client\]?\"       \"We can use it as a starting point, but each deployment is configured for your workflows. The meeting is specifically to understand where you differ from the default.\"
  -------------------------------------------------------------- ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

**Appendix A --- Mock Sales Narrative (for practice)**

**Client:** Bright Trading Sdn Bhd

**Industry:** FMCG wholesale distribution

**Size:** \~30 staff, 5 in sales, 3 in warehouse, 1 finance

**Current system:** Manual WhatsApp + Excel. Sales reps take orders via WhatsApp, type them into Excel, and email the SO to warehouse. Warehouse prints and picks. Invoices created manually in Excel by finance.

**Key pain:** Orders keyed twice (WhatsApp → Excel → email). No real-time stock visibility. Customers complain about delays and errors.

**What they want from MAIA:** Automate order capture from WhatsApp, generate SO automatically, warehouse gets notified instantly, auto-generate invoice on delivery.

**Integrations:** Currently using AutoCount for accounting. Want MAIA to sync invoices and payments.

**Volume:** \~50 orders/day, \~300 SKUs, \~150 active customers

**Timeline pressure:** Wants to go live before Raya (4 weeks from now)

**Budget signal:** Has budget approved. Signed off by director.
