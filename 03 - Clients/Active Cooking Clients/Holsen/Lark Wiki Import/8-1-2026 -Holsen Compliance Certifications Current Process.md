# **C1 Process** 

## **What C1 is used for (in practice)**

C1 is offered to customers who purchase **manufactured goods**. Some customers use it, some do not.

### **What Holsen needs from the customer**

Holsen requests the following from the customer:

- The **latest C1 certificate**
- The customer’s **item list**
- The **tariff code(s)** relevant to manufactured goods

### **Step-by-step workflow**

1. **The customer applies for C1** through the customer portal.
2. **Customer sends Holsen**:

   - C1 certificate (latest copy)
   - Item list
3. **Holsen verifies** the certificate by checking:

   - Whether the relevant **tariff code(s)** are included/covered.
4. **Holsen stores the C1 certificate digitally** under the customer:

   - Only **one active/latest C1** is kept
   - Older versions are **archived**
5. **When the customer places an order**:

   - Holsen checks whether the customer has an active **C1**.
   - Holsen checks whether the ordered manufactured goods match the **tariff code coverage**.
   - If covered, the order lines can be treated as **tax exempt**.

### **C1 decision rules (as stated)**

- C1 exemption depends on **tariff code coverage**.
- Holsen mentioned they have **two general tariff codes**, so a customer may have coverage for one and not the other.
- In general (their wording): if a customer has C1 and tariff code coverage, manufactured items can be tax exempt.

# **C3 Process** 

## **What makes C3 different**

C3 is not a general flag like “this item is C3”.

C3 must be tracked as:

- **Item first**
- Then **which customer**
- And the **quantity/quota** approved



So it is effectively: **“C3 for Customer A for Item X (with quantity)”**

## **What Holsen needs from the customer**

For C3, the customer must provide:

- A **Purchase Order (PO)**
- The **C3 certificate** (after approval)

## **Step-by-step workflow (as stated)**

1. **Customer issues a PO** for the product.
2. Customer uses that **PO to apply for the C3 exemption/license**.
3. **The customer receives the approved C3 certificate** and sends it to Holsen.
4. Holsen uses the **PO + certificate** to:

   - Reserve the goods under C3 for that customer
   - Deduct/reserve inventory based on the approved quantity/quota
5. When fulfilling the order, Holsen checks in this order:

   - **Item first**
   - Then whether the **customer has C3 approval for that item**
   - Then whether the requested **quantity is within the approved quota**

## **C3 decision rule (as stated)**

- C3 tagging cannot be done as “Item 2 is C3” in a generic way.
- It must always be “Item X is C3 **for Customer A**”.

# **COA Process** 

## **What COA is used for (in practice)**

COA is a certificate tied to product/batches, and it is sent based on what the customer requires.

## **Step-by-step workflow (as stated)**

1. **Determine customer requirement first**:

   - Does this customer require COA or not?
2. If yes, confirm:

   - **What format does** the customer wants COA in
   - **What information** must be included
3. During **batch ingestion into inventory**:

   - Holsen must **tag/attach the COA to the batch**
4. During **order fulfillment / dispatch**:

   - Surface the **relevant COA** in the customer-required format.
