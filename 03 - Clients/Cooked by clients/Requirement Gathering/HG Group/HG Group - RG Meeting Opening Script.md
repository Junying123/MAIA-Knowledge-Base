# HG Group — RG Meeting Opening Script

**Host:** Gareth
**Support:** [Supervisor name]
**Session duration:** ~90 minutes
**Purpose:** Discovery — understand how HG operates and where the process breaks down

---

> Adapt naturally — don't read verbatim if it feels stiff, but hit every beat in order.

---

## Step 1 — Welcome and Introduce the Room

> "Hi [name], thank you for making the time. My name is Gareth, I'm a Product Manager at Mindhive. Joining me today is [supervisor name] — [he/she] leads our product and implementation work and is here to support the conversation.
>
> We're really glad to be here. We've done a bit of homework on HG before this session — looked through your website, your service divisions, how your engagement process works. So we come in with some context, which hopefully means we can use this time well rather than just covering basics."

---

## Step 2 — Set the Agenda and Tone

> "Here's what we'd like to do today. This isn't a sales pitch — not yet. What we want to do first is understand how HG actually operates day-to-day. The process on the website is clear. What's less clear to us — and what we're hoping you'll help us understand — is where that process gets hard to manage at the volume you're running.
>
> We'll ask some questions, you talk, we listen. There are no wrong answers — the more honest and specific you are, the better we can figure out whether what we've built is actually useful for you or not.
>
> We've set aside about 90 minutes. Does that still work on your end?"

---

## Step 3 — Brief Credibility Anchor

> "Just so you know who you're talking to — Mindhive built MAIA, which is a business operations platform that we've deployed with companies across Malaysia in manufacturing, distribution, and services. We work with clients like [mention one relevant name if appropriate]. What we're exploring today is whether the same platform — or a version of it shaped for HG — could make a real difference to how your operations run."

---

## Step 4 — Hand the Floor to Them

> "Before we jump into questions — is there anything you'd like to know about us first, or anything you're hoping to get out of today's session?"

**→ Listen carefully here.**
- If they say *"we want to understand pricing"* or *"we're evaluating a few options"* — note it, it tells you their mindset going in. Acknowledge it and come back to it at the end.
- If they say *"we just want to see if this can help us"* — great, dive straight into questions.
- If they ask a product question — give a one-line answer and redirect: *"Great question — let me come back to that once we understand your setup a bit better."*

---

## Step 5 — Transition to Question 1

> "Perfect. Let's start from the top — I'd love to understand what a normal day looks like for you."

**→ Move to the RG Questionnaire, Question 1.**

---

## Supervisor's Role During the Session

- **Let Gareth lead** — questions, transitions, and pacing
- **Probe deeper** when something interesting surfaces, e.g.:
  - *"Can I follow up on that — when you say it goes through WhatsApp, is there a specific group for that, or direct messages?"*
  - *"You mentioned that takes a few days — how often does that happen?"*
  - *"Is that something one person manages, or does the whole team deal with it?"*
- **Take notes** on pain signals, specific numbers, and any scope implications
- **Catch what's missed** — if a key question from the checklist wasn't covered, add at the end: *"One more thing before we wrap — [question]"*
- **Don't talk over Gareth** — wait for a natural pause before jumping in

---

## If the Session Runs Long

If you're approaching 75 minutes and haven't hit the Must-Confirm checklist yet, say:

> "We're coming up on time — I want to make sure we cover a few important things before we close. Can I run through a quick checklist?"

Then go directly to the **Must-Confirm Before Leaving** section in the RG Questionnaire.

---

## Closing the Session

Once questions are done and must-confirms are checked, close with:

> "This has been really helpful — genuinely. We now have a much clearer picture of how HG operates and where the friction is. Here's what happens next:
>
> We'll take everything from today and put together a proper proposal — what we'd build, how it maps to your workflow, and what it would cost. We'll share that with you within [X working days].
>
> Before we go — we'd love a few sample documents from you to make the proposal feel real and specific to HG rather than generic. Things like a recent quote, a completion report, any job brief you send to team leads. Even a WhatsApp screenshot is fine. Can we collect those before we leave today, or would you be able to send them over this week?"

**→ Move to the Demo Sample Requests section in the RG Questionnaire.**
