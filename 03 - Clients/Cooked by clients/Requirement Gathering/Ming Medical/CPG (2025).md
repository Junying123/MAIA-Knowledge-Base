# CPG (2025) — Ming Medical Cell Therapy Protocol

> **Updated: 1st June 2025**
>
> ⚠️ Elderly patients require more applications of stem cells and/or exosomes, and regeneration will take 2–4× longer to repair.

---

## Therapy Types

- **Stem Cell (SC)** — IV bolus or targeted injection
- **Exosomes** — Eye-drops, Nebulizer, HydroGel, 200FU IV, Intraarticular (with HA)
- **NK Cells** — Minimum 10–30 infusions depending on stage

## Cancers

### Brain / CNS (Solid Tumours & GBM)

| Stage | Administration | Dose | Frequency |
|---|---|---|---|
| Late-stage comatose (ICU) | Transcranial (preferred) or IV bolus | 1,000 FU NK-Exos | 1× dose |
| GBM — post craniotomy, post radio, post chemo | Transcranial NK Cell infusion; intrathecal infusion | 200–400 mil NK cells daily | 10× doses (early stage); 20× (early mets); 30× (late stage) |

### Peripheral System Cancers

| Stage | Administration | Dose | Frequency |
|---|---|---|---|
| Early stage (no mets) | IV bolus; intratumoral where applicable | 200 mil cell/dose | 10× doses |
| Stage 3–4 (multiple mets), post resection/radio/chemo, palliative | IV bolus; intratumoral subject to tumour site and load | 200–400 mil cells daily | 20× (early mets); 30× (late stage); 40+ (turbo cancer) |

### Cancers with Limited NK Cell Efficacy

| Condition | Notes | Possible Alternative | Frequency |
|---|---|---|---|

| Osteosarcomas | Same as above | — | — |
| Severe-stage lung / late-stage 'turbo' solid cancers | NK-Exos penetrate but will inflict pain; risk of total lung collapse after cancer cleared | — | — |

- Minimum 10–30 infusions for cancer treatment
