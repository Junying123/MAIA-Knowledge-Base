# GTM Briefs

High-level prospect intelligence prepared by the GTM team **before** any PM-led discovery meeting.

## Purpose

The GTM Brief gives the PM enough context to run a smart discovery call — without going into product-level detail yet. It captures:
- Who the prospect is and where they came from
- Their top pain points (as understood by GTM)
- A rough sense of their business workflows
- An initial gut-check on product fit

**This is GTM's handover to PM.** The PM reads it before the discovery call, not after.

## How to Use

1. GTM creates a new file: `[Prospect Name].md`
2. Copy `[[02 - PM Playbook/Templates/[Template] GTM Brief]]`
3. Fill in all sections from your pre-sales intel (calls, emails, referral notes)
4. Tag the PM owner and notify them it's ready
5. PM reviews before the discovery call
