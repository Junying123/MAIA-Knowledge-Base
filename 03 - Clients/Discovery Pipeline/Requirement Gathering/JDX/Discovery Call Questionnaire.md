# JDX — Discovery Call Questionnaire

> **Read first:** [[03 - Clients/Discovery Pipeline/GTM Briefs/JDX/JDX]] — GTM brief with company context and initial fit hypothesis.

**Call date:** TBC
**PM:** [Name]
**Attendees:** [Names]

Use this checklist during the call. Check off each item as it's answered. Leave blanks for follow-up.

---

## 1. Sales Channel & Order Intake

*Diagram shows: Sales Channel → 90% WhatsApp / 5% Email → Sales Order. Many unknowns remain.*

- [ ] Walk us through how a customer places an order — what's the exact flow from first contact to confirmed order?
- [ ] The diagram shows 90% via WhatsApp, 5% via Email — what's the remaining ~5%? Phone call? In-person?
- [ ] Who handles incoming orders — a dedicated CS person, or the sales agent themselves?
- [ ] How many sales orders do you process on average per month?
- [ ] Do customers send a formal PO, or is it just a WhatsApp message with a list?
- [ ] Do you issue a quotation before the sales order, or go straight to SO?
- [ ] How is the sales order currently created — manually keyed into the ERP, or does the CS team do it?
- [ ] Do walk-in / self-collect customers order in advance or on the spot?

---

## 2. Customer Service & Order Processing

*Diagram shows CS handling PO % and messages, inventory check in Autocount. ERP/accounting system is unknown.*

- [ ] What system does the CS team use today to manage orders? (ERP name? Autocount?)
- [ ] The diagram shows "inventory check happens in Autocount" — is Autocount your current ERP, or just accounting?
- [ ] What other systems are in use? (separate inventory system, salesman app, spreadsheets?)
- [ ] How does CS confirm stock availability before confirming the order to the customer?
- [ ] What happens if an item is out of stock — do you back-order, substitute, or cancel?
- [ ] How do you currently communicate order status back to the customer (WhatsApp, email, call)?
- [ ] Who is the CS team — how many people, and what are their roles?

---

## 3. Pricing

*Diagram flags: tier pricing? promotion? region pricing? special customer pricing? — all unconfirmed.*

- [ ] Do you have different price tiers for different customer types (e.g. wholesale vs retail)?
- [ ] Do you offer region-based pricing (different states / areas)?
- [ ] Do you run promotions or seasonal discounts? How are these applied — manual or system?
- [ ] Do any customers have individually negotiated prices (special customer pricing)?
- [ ] How is the price list managed today — Excel, ERP, or something else?
- [ ] Do sales agents have the authority to give ad hoc discounts? Is there an approval process?

---

## 4. Finance & Payment

*Diagram shows two paths: Credit Customer and Cash. Payment terms and bank recon steps have open questions.*

### Credit Customers
- [ ] What is the credit limit assessment process — who decides, and on what basis?
- [ ] What happens when a customer hits their credit limit? Can it be increased? Who approves?
- [ ] If a customer can't pay, how long before the SO is cancelled?
- [ ] How do you track outstanding AR — in the ERP, Excel, or manually?

### Payment Terms
- [ ] What payment terms do you offer?
  - Cash upfront before delivery?
  - Cash within X days after delivery?
  - Credit terms — 60 days? 90 days? 120 days?
- [ ] Can a single customer have multiple payment term arrangements?
- [ ] Do customers need to pay before the SO is released to the warehouse?

### Payment Collection & Reconciliation
- [ ] How does a customer notify you of payment — bank slip via WhatsApp? Email?
- [ ] How do you reconcile payments — manually match bank statement to invoice?
- [ ] Who does the bank reconciliation, and how often?
- [ ] Do customers ever partially pay an invoice?

---

## 5. Warehouse & Delivery

*Diagram shows: own collection vs managed delivery, successful/failed delivery handling, DHL & others unknown.*

### Delivery
- [ ] What % of orders are self-collect vs you arrange delivery?
- [ ] For deliveries you manage — do you use your own drivers, third-party (DHL, others), or both?
- [ ] How do you currently schedule and assign deliveries?
- [ ] What happens when a delivery fails (customer unavailable)? How is it rescheduled?
- [ ] When the customer is unavailable, the driver takes a photo — is this a current process or a desired one?
- [ ] Does the customer sign a physical DO, or is proof of delivery done digitally?

### Delivery Order (DO) Process
- [ ] When is the DO generated — before or after the warehouse picks and packs?
- [ ] Who sends the DO to the warehouse team — CS or the system automatically?
- [ ] How does the warehouse team currently receive the SO/DO (printed, WhatsApp, system)?

---

## 6. Returns & Credit Notes

*Diagram shows ~5% return rate. Two credit note types: regular item vs custom board.*

- [ ] What is your actual return rate, and what are the most common reasons?
- [ ] Walk us through a return — customer contacts who? What's the process?
- [ ] How do you determine if a returned item is damaged or not?
  - If not damaged → product is exchanged. Is this a same-day swap or a new order?
  - If damaged → inventory adjusted and product disposed. Who approves disposal?
- [ ] The diagram shows two types of credit notes: "regular item" and "custom board" — what's the difference?
- [ ] How are credit notes currently issued — in the ERP, manually, or via a separate tool?
- [ ] Can a credit note offset against a future invoice, or is it always a cash refund?

---

## 7. Inventory & Forecasting

*JDX-specific — flagged in GTM brief as a major pain point.*

- [ ] Walk us through how you decide how much stock to put in each outlet (Giant, AEON, bottle shops)
- [ ] Is forecasting truly by gut feel, or is there an Excel / data file behind it?
- [ ] How far in advance do you plan your stock allocation? Weekly? Monthly?
- [ ] How do you handle seasonal spikes (e.g. Chinese New Year)? Is there a documented process?
- [ ] Do you track sell-through per outlet, or just total sales?
- [ ] What triggers a replenishment to an outlet — a fixed schedule, or when stock hits a threshold?
- [ ] Have you ever run out of stock at an outlet unexpectedly? How was it handled?

---

## 8. Consignment Workflow

*JDX-specific — non-standard, flagged as potential customisation need.*

- [ ] Walk us through the full consignment cycle with Giant/AEON end-to-end:
  - How do you decide quantity to place at each outlet?
  - How do you track what's placed vs what's sold?
  - How often do you reconcile sell-through?
  - How is the invoice triggered — on placement, on sell-through, or periodically?
- [ ] Who physically replenishes stock at the outlet — your team or theirs?
- [ ] Do Giant/AEON send you a sell-through report, or do you go in to check?
- [ ] For bottle shops (salesman direct sales):
  - The salesman visits in person and issues an invoice on the spot — is payment also collected on the spot?
  - What app does the salesman use today? What does it do well / poorly?
  - Does the salesman app sync back to your ERP? Or is it disconnected?
- [ ] Are there any other consignment customers beyond Giant and AEON?

---

## 9. Current Systems & Integrations

- [ ] Full list of systems currently in use (ERP, accounting, salesman app, spreadsheets, etc.)
- [ ] Which system is the source of truth for inventory? For finance?
- [ ] Are any of these systems integrated today, or is data transferred manually?
- [ ] What are the biggest pain points with the current system setup?
- [ ] Is there an intention to keep the salesman app, or would you want MAIA to replace it?
- [ ] What accounting system do you use — Autocount? SQL? Xero? Is the finance team open to changing it?
