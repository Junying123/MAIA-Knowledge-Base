This document explains the three external costs associated with running MAIA for your business. These are third-party provider charges billed directly to your company by Meta, OpenAI, and AWS. They are not charged, marked up, or collected by Mindhive or MAIA. The estimates below use a sample baseline of 600 orders per month to help you understand the likely monthly budget required. One "order" means one complete order process from intake through to invoice generation.
1
WhatsApp Business API (WABA)
Charged by Meta — pay per message sent outside a conversation window
How WABA charging works: When a customer messages your MAIA number first, Meta opens a free 24-hour service window. All replies and messages within that window — including MAIA's responses and document notifications — are free of charge. You are only charged when MAIA sends a message to a customer outside that 24-hour window, or sends a marketing message. For businesses where most orders are customer-initiated and processed promptly, the majority of conversations usually fall within the free window.

2
OpenAI — AI Token Usage
Charged by OpenAI — pay per AI task performed
What are tokens? Every time MAIA reads a message, extracts order details, matches SKUs, or prepares a document, it uses AI — and AI usage is measured in "tokens" (think of them as small units of text processed). You purchase a credit balance with OpenAI, and each action deducts a small amount. For reference, one complete order process from WhatsApp message to invoice generation costs approximately RM 0.16 – RM 0.39 in tokens, depending on complexity.

3
AWS — Server & Data Hosting
Charged by Amazon Web Services — your data lives here
What is AWS for? Amazon Web Services (AWS) is where MAIA's data is hosted — your orders, documents, customer records, and inventory data are all stored and processed here. It also handles the connection between MAIA and your SQL Accounting system. Think of it as the secure server room that keeps MAIA running. The cost is shared across all infrastructure components that support your account.
