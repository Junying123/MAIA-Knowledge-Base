## 2. Core Business Workflow — The Retail Lot Lifecycle

Every retail lot inside a shopping mall goes through 8 stages — from when a tenant signs a lease to when they open their doors (and eventually hand the space back). HG plugs into **3 of those 8 stages** as the specialist contractor.

### The 8-Stage Retail Lot Lifecycle

| Stage | What Happens |
|---|---|
| Stage 1 | **Fit-Out** — Tenant renovates the raw lot into their store (painting, tiling, partitions, ceiling, scaffold) |
| Stage 2 | Store opens and begins trading |
| Stage 3 | Store is trading normally |
| Stage 4 | Tenant decides to exit / lease ends |
| Stage 5 | **Hoarding & Visual** — Lot is closed off; hoarding goes up to block public view while works happen |
| Stage 6 | **Reinstatement** — Tenant's fit-out is demolished; lot is restored to original bare condition for handover back to mall |
| Stage 7 | Mall re-lets the lot to a new tenant |
| Stage 8 | New tenant begins their own fit-out (back to Stage 1) |

### Where HG Fits In

HG is the contractor called in at the three most operationally complex stages:

- **Stage 5 — Hoarding & Visual:** HG installs hoarding panels overnight to close off the lot from public view. Titan-certified, PE-endorsed, mall-compliant. Also wraps the hoarding with printed graphics (brand reveals, coming-soon visuals) for the incoming tenant.
- **Stage 6 — Reinstatement & Restoration:** HG demolishes everything the outgoing tenant built — floors, ceilings, walls, fit-out — and returns the lot to its original bare handover condition. Mall-compliant make-good, on time.
- **Stage 1 — Fit-Out Support:** HG supports the incoming tenant's renovation contractor with the trade work they can't self-perform — painting, tiling, partition walls, plaster ceiling, and scaffold access.

### Add-On Services (Across All Stages)

These run alongside any stage as needed:

- **Scaffold** — Certified aluminium scaffold for work-at-height access during fit-out or reinstatement
- **Lorry & Rorobin** — Licensed construction waste removal and materials transport
- **Temporary Storage** — Short-term storage for tenant items during the transition between stages

> **In plain terms:** When a shop closes down or a new one opens in a mall, HG is the team that puts up the barriers, strips out the old fit-out, and helps build the new one. One contractor handles the messy middle that most people never see.

## 3. Products & Services (8 Service Divisions)

HG operates 8 specialist service divisions, all fully in-house — no subcontracting.

### 1. Hoarding Services
- Supply and installation of construction hoarding for mall retail lots, kiosks, and outdoor construction/project sites
- Full lifecycle coverage: new installation, modification (push-out/push-in), rectification, and dismantling
- Authorised Malaysian distributor of the Titan Hoarding System (Australia) — AS 4687 engineer-certified, free-standing, requires zero floor or ceiling fixings
- PE-endorsed engineering drawings submitted for every job; mall permit applications handled by HG
- Supports 6 project types, 8 board materials (MDF, PVC plywood, gypsum, marine ply, metal deck, GI sheet), 4 structure options, 7 door configurations
- Overnight installation capability — same-day mobilisation available

### 2. Printing & Visual Services
- Large-format graphic printing and installation for hoarding wraps, glass/window stickers, wall decals, tarpaulin banners, and escalator stickers
- Used for pre-opening teasers, brand reveals, and coming-soon visuals on mall hoarding
- Print technologies: eco-solvent (indoor) and UV-cured (outdoor, scratch-resistant)
- 8 material types: stickers (hoarding/glass/wall), tarpaulin canvas, white tarpaulin, frosted sticker, translucent glass sticker, tension fabric poster, one-way vision sticker
- In-house print and install — no split vendor between printer and installer
- Turnaround from 48 hours; colour-matched to brand guidelines; lamination options available

### 3. Scaffold Services
- Aluminium scaffold rental and installation via the BS Scaffold division
- Types: ringlock scaffold (aluminium), modular scaffold (mobile tower, cantilever/escalator access, bridge, slim), and metal A-frame scaffold
- Used as working platforms for construction, fit-out, and all work-at-height tasks
- JKKP-certified at Levels 1, 2 & 3 (Scaffold Competency)
- Green Tag endorsement available for installed scaffolds: Work-at-Height and JKKP Scaffold Inspector certification

### 4. Reinstatement & Restoration Services
- Full demolition and make-good service to return a renovated premise to its original handover condition
- Handled by the H.O.R.R (Home & Office Reinstatement & Restoration) division
- Covers mall retail lots, office lots, commercial shop lots, and residential premises
- Scope: demolition, debris removal, floor hacking and screeding, ceiling restoration, wall plastering and painting, M&E reinstatement (electrical, sprinkler, AC/ducting), and structural restoration
- Coordinates with mall panel contractors for M&E items (sprinkler, LPG, wet chemical systems) outside HG's direct scope
- Compliant with individual mall/building management requirements

### 5. Subcontractor (Fit-Out) Services
- Wet work and interior build-out via the SCS Group (Subcon Services) and Tiles Replacement Services (TRS) divisions
- Partition wall installation (stud/drywall type)
- Plaster ceiling: plain, L-box, and light trough configurations
- Tiling and tiles replacement: marble, granite, terrazzo, terracotta, standard tiles — new installation, rectification, and replacement
- Cement screeding and flooring
- Brick wall erection with stiffener and lintel structures
- Painting: indoor and outdoor
- Site protection installation
- Waterproofing: membrane torch-on application
- All work performed by in-house teams only

### 6. Signage Services
- Building facade and indoor signage fabrication and installation by the HG Signage division
- Types: back-lit signage, alphabet/letter-box signage, aluminium box signage, general facade signage
- Also handles signage repair/rectification and dismantling
- Crane and sky-lift rental available for high-elevation installations
- Local authority permit applications managed (road closure, road diversion where required)

### 7. Lorry & Rorobin Services
- Day-and-night lorry transport and licensed rorobin (skip bin) rental via the RRB and HG Lorry divisions
- Lorry fleet: 3-tonne and 5-tonne 17-footer box trucks with tail lifts
- Rorobin bins in 3 sizes: 6'×12'×2' (heavy debris — concrete, sand, brick), 6'×12'×4' and 6'×12'×5' (light/medium debris — gypsum, timber, glass)
- Services: mover/relocation, lorry delivery, and licensed construction waste removal
- Operates within KL and Selangor

### 8. Temporary Storage Services
- Short-term storage space (TLB — Tempat Letak Barang) at HG's Bandar Kinrara warehouse facility
- Used to store client items during reinstatement/renovation before the new space is ready
- Also holds items temporarily while renovation is underway, then returns them to the lot on completion
- No deposit required; customisable space size based on client need
- Forklift, manpower, and lorry available on-site for loading/unloading
- Serves KL and Selangor clients

## 6. Customer Problems

- **Coordinating multiple specialist contractors:** Mall fit-out and reinstatement projects typically require separate contractors for hoarding, scaffold, reinstatement, printing, and logistics — creating coordination overhead, communication gaps, and quality inconsistency.
- **Tight timelines and overnight installation requirements:** Retail mall environments demand hoarding to go up overnight with minimal disruption to shoppers and mall operations. Most contractors cannot reliably execute this at speed.
- **Mall compliance complexity:** Shopping malls have strict hoarding and site safety requirements. Contractors unfamiliar with mall standards risk non-compliance, delays, and rejection from mall management.
- **Urgent mobilisation needs:** Retail projects are often time-sensitive — lease handover dates, tenant opening dates, and construction windows create unpredictable demand spikes that require same-day response.
- **Construction waste and logistics management:** Managing rorobin bins, lorry transport, and temporary storage are often overlooked logistics that main contractors need to outsource to a licensed, reliable party.

## 7. Solutions Provided

- **Single-contractor model:** HG covers hoarding, scaffold, reinstatement, printing, signage, fit-out support, lorry, and storage under one roof — one point of contact, one contract, consistent quality across all services.
- **Overnight hoarding installation capability:** Dedicated teams, equipment on permanent standby, and day/night shift operations (7 days a week) allow HG to execute time-critical mall installations without disruption.
- **Mall compliance expertise:** 10 years and 100+ mall relationships (including Pavilion and Suria KLCC) give HG deep familiarity with individual mall compliance standards — reducing rejection risk for clients.
- **Same-day mobilisation:** Manpower, lorries, and materials are maintained on permanent standby for urgent same-day deployment — critical for retail fit-out emergencies and tight handover windows.
- **Licensed waste and logistics services:** As a licensed RRB (rorobin bin) provider and lorry operator, HG handles construction waste removal and materials transport compliantly, removing logistics burden from main contractors.
