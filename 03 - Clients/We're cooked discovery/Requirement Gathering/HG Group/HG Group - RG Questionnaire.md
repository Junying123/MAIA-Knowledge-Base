# HG Group — Requirement Gathering Questionnaire

**Client:** HG Services (M) Sdn Bhd
**Session target:** 2 hours — discovery, not audit
**Prepared by:** Mindhive PM team

---

> **How to run this session.**
> We already know HG's 7-step process and 8-division structure — don't re-confirm it. Every question below is designed to get HG talking about where the process breaks down. Ask one question, then stop and listen. If the answer is short, follow up with **"Can you walk me through a recent example?"** — stories surface pain faster than any question.
>
> Target: **25 questions across ~90 minutes**, leaving 30 minutes for demo sample requests and wrap-up.

---

## What We Already Know

| Step | What Happens |
|---|---|
| 1 | Enquiry received (WhatsApp / call / web form) |
| 2 | Site visit / measurement |
| 3 | Quotation sent (within 2 hours) |
| 4 | Client confirmation |
| 5 | Permit application to mall |
| 6 | Job execution on-site |
| 7 | Completion report within 48 hours |

8 items required from client before Step 3 can proceed: lot number, mall name, job drawings, start date, work permit copy, mall contact, insurance cover note, payment slip.

---

## Opening — How the Business Actually Runs (10 min)

**1.** Walk me through what a typical day looks like for you — what does the morning look like, and what does end-of-day look like?

**2.** If I called you right now and asked "what's the status of the reinstatement job at Pavilion?" — how would you find that answer?

**3.** What's the one part of running operations day-to-day that you find most exhausting — not the hardest technically, just the most draining to keep on top of?

> Listen for: WhatsApp chasing, jobs tracked in someone's head, coordinators as the single point of failure.

---

## Job Intake & Document Chase (15 min)

**4.** When a new job comes in, walk me through what happens from the moment the enquiry arrives to the moment you're ready to send the quote — who does what, and what goes back and forth with the client?

**5.** You need up to 8 items from a client before the job can properly move forward. What happens on the days when a client sends documents one at a time over three days — how do you track what's still outstanding across all your active jobs?

**6.** How many jobs right now are stuck waiting on something from a client? Can you tell me that number without having to check?

> Listen for: No checklist, mental tracking, jobs stalling because one document is missing.

---

## Quoting & Pricing (15 min)

**7.** Walk me through the last quote your team sent — who prepared it, what did it look like, and where does the copy live now?

**8.** If a client came back six weeks later and said "this invoice doesn't match what we agreed" — how would you find the original quote?

**9.** When you quote a job — hoarding, reinstatement, scaffold — are you working from a standard rate card or pricing it from scratch each time?

**10.** When preparing a quote, how are materials factored in — is there a standard Bill of Materials per job type (e.g. a 20-lm hoarding job always needs X panels, Y boards), or does someone work it out fresh each time?

> Listen for: Quotes living in WhatsApp, no searchable history, material costs estimated informally, no BOM template.

---

## Multi-Division Jobs & Sequencing (20 min)

**11.** Tell me about the most complex job HG has run recently — one that involved multiple divisions. How did you keep all the divisions in sync?

**12.** When scaffold finishes and the reinstatement team needs to know the site is ready — what's the actual trigger for that handoff? Who tells who, and through what channel?

**13.** Has a downstream team ever shown up to a site and found the upstream division hadn't finished? Walk me through what happened and what it cost.

**14.** When a multi-division job is running, is there one person who can see the full picture across all divisions — or does each team lead only know their own piece?

> Listen for: WhatsApp as the handoff mechanism, one coordinator holding everything in their head, wasted mobilisations.

---

## Job Work Order: Before, During & After (15 min)

**15.** Before a team lead goes on site — what information do they actually have in hand when they arrive? How does that reach them?

**16.** When scope changes on site — extra work, extra materials used, client adds something on the day — how does that get captured and communicated back to the office?

**17.** Site photos: how do they get from the field back to the office, and where do they end up? Are they filed against a specific job record or do they sit in a WhatsApp group?

**18.** If a coordinator was sick mid-job, how quickly could someone else pick it up? Where would they look to understand what's been done and what's still outstanding?

> Listen for: Team lead "just knows", photos in group chat, deviations absorbed informally, no handover-ready job record.

---

## Completion Reports & Invoicing (15 min)

**19.** Walk me through how a completion report gets made — who does it, how long does it take, and what happens on a day when 5 jobs complete at the same time?

**20.** After the completion report goes out — how long does it typically take for the invoice to follow? What's the process in between?

**21.** If I asked you today how much money is owed to HG across all outstanding invoices — could you tell me that number? How would you find it?

**22.** Has a client ever disputed an invoice because what was charged didn't match what was originally agreed? How was it resolved?

> Listen for: Manual report compilation, invoicing lag, invisible receivables, disputes from untracked variations.

---

## Client Relationships & Institutional Memory (10 min)

**23.** For your most important repeat clients — the ones who give HG work across multiple malls — how much of that relationship lives in a coordinator's head versus somewhere the whole team can access?

**24.** If your most experienced coordinator handed in their notice tomorrow, what would happen to the client relationships and job history they were managing?

> Listen for: Context walking out the door, no CRM, relationships owned by individuals not the business.

---

## Closing — The Ceiling (5 min)

**25.** HG has gone from one install in 2018 to 20+ a day today. If you doubled volume again — what's the first thing in your operation that would break?

> Listen for: "The coordination would break," "we'd need more coordinators," "the WhatsApp system can't scale." This is the opening for MAIA.

---

## Must-Confirm Before Leaving

Don't close the session without answers to these. Weave them into the conversation — not a rapid-fire list at the end.

- [ ] **Real job volume** — actual daily count across all 8 divisions today
- [ ] **Document intake method** — how are the 8 items collected? WhatsApp, form, email?
- [ ] **Pricing structure** — rate card or ad hoc per job?
- [ ] **BOM** — standard material list per job type, or worked out fresh each time? Planned vs actual tracked?
- [ ] **Job brief format** — what goes to the team lead before a job? Request a sample
- [ ] **Deviation capture** — how are scope changes on site recorded and fed back to the office?
- [ ] **Client sign-off** — formal sign-off on site or just a WhatsApp "done"?
- [ ] **Completion report format** — request a sample PDF
- [ ] **Invoicing software** — SQL, Excel, manual?
- [ ] **Permit tracking** — how is permit status tracked across 20+ active jobs?
- [ ] **Division sequencing** — written down or tribal knowledge?
- [ ] **Role access needs** — who should see what; who should NOT see pricing/financials?
- [ ] **Decision-maker** — who has sign-off authority for a system implementation?

---

## Demo Sample Requests

Ask at the end. Frame it as making the demo feel real.

> "To show MAIA using your actual data — not generic examples — we'd love a few things before demo day."

- [ ] A recent quote (PDF or screenshot)
- [ ] A completion report example (current PDF format)
- [ ] A multi-division job walkthrough (recent job touching 3+ divisions)
- [ ] Any current job brief or Work Order format used with team leads
- [ ] A WhatsApp thread showing the 8-item document collection from a client
