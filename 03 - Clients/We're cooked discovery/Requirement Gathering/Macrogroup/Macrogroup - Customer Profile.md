| Industry | Electronic components distribution; contract electronics manufacturing (EMS) |
| Founded | 1994 (British holding opened Russian branch); 2001 (became independent Russian company) |
| Headquarters | Saint Petersburg, Russia — Sveaborgskaya Street, 12, 196105 |
| Geographic Reach | Russia and CIS countries; regional offices in Moscow, Yekaterinburg, Novosibirsk, Cheboksary, Rostov-on-Don |
| Website | https://www.macrogroup.biz (also macrogroup.ru) |
| Company Type | Private Russian LLC (ООО); ~118 employees; estimated ~$30M revenue |

Macro Group is one of Russia's leading distributors of electronic components and modules, serving Russian and CIS OEMs across defense, aerospace, telecoms, and industrial sectors. Originally a branch of a British holding company, Macro Group became an independent Russian entity in 2001 and has since built an authorised distribution network with 20+ global component manufacturers, complemented by its own in-house contract electronics manufacturing arm (Macro EMS) in Saint Petersburg.

## 5. Customer Problems

- **Component access and sourcing reliability:** Russian OEMs designing with advanced components (FPGAs, RF ICs, power semiconductors) depend on a limited pool of authorised distributors. Post-2022 sanctions have disrupted direct manufacturer channels, making trusted distributors with alternative sourcing critical for production continuity.

- **Counterfeit and grey-market risk:** The electronic components market — particularly in Russia — is vulnerable to counterfeit parts. OEM procurement teams need verified, certified sources to protect product quality and compliance with military, aerospace, or medical standards.

- **Technical complexity of advanced components:** Components like FPGAs and RF modules require deep engineering expertise to evaluate, select, and integrate. OEM engineers often lack vendor-side application support, creating design risk and delays without a knowledgeable distributor partner.

- **Prototype-to-production gap:** Engineering firms can design a product but struggle to scale from prototype to reliable batch or mass production without access to EMS partners who understand both the components and the assembly process.

- **Supply chain fragmentation across multiple vendors:** OEMs sourcing components from 20+ manufacturers face complexity managing relationships, lead times, documentation, and quality claims across each separately — creating administrative overhead and single points of failure.

- **Long procurement and design cycles:** In industries like defense and aerospace, procurement cycles are extended and decisions are risk-averse. Companies need a distributor that can provide technical validation, samples, reference designs, and software tools early in the design phase to shorten time-to-market.

## 6. Solutions Provided

- **Authorised multi-brand distribution:** Macro Group holds authorised distributor status with 20+ global manufacturers (Xilinx, Broadcom, Qorvo, SIMCom, Power Integrations, etc.), giving customers access to verified, genuine components with full manufacturer traceability and warranty.

- **Parallel import supply:** Following 2022 sanctions, Macro Group operates alternative supply channels alongside authorised routes, providing continuity for Russian OEMs that can no longer source directly from Western manufacturers.

- **Full-lifecycle technical support:** From component selection and design consultation through to debugging and in-house claims analysis, Macro Group's engineering team supports customers across the entire product development cycle — reducing design risk and accelerating time-to-market.

- **In-house contract electronics manufacturing (Macro EMS):** Customers can outsource PCB assembly and production directly to Macro Group, with two automated lines capable of 120,000 components/hour — bridging the prototype-to-production gap within a single supplier relationship.

- **One-stop component portfolio:** By aggregating 20+ manufacturers' product lines, Macro Group acts as a single point of contact for the bulk of a customer's component needs, reducing vendor fragmentation and procurement complexity.

- **Certified supply for regulated industries:** ISO 9001 certification, military supplier approval, and Ministry of Nuclear Energy certification allow Macro Group to serve customers in defense, nuclear, and aerospace — sectors that cannot accept uncertified supply chains.
