## 5. Customer Problems

- **Inconsistent product quality from suppliers:** F&B operators in Malaysia commonly face variable quality in frozen protein — different grades arriving under the same product name, or inconsistent thaw performance. Macro Frozen's "We taste before we sell" positioning directly addresses this pain point.

- **Unreliable supply during peak demand periods:** Restaurants and caterers face stock shortages during high-demand periods (weekends, festive seasons) when their primary suppliers fail to fulfil orders on time, disrupting kitchen operations and service.

- **Sourcing premium-grade frozen protein at scale:** Small-to-mid-sized F&B businesses struggle to access premium quality frozen meat and poultry at wholesale prices — larger distributors often prioritise large volume customers, leaving SME operators underserved.

- **Cold chain integrity concerns:** F&B buyers worry about whether frozen products have been properly maintained throughout the supply chain. A single temperature break can render a batch unusable, increasing food cost risk.

- **Order management friction:** Many small frozen food suppliers in Malaysia operate informally via WhatsApp and phone calls, making it difficult for procurement teams to track orders, confirm delivery status, manage invoices, and maintain purchase history.

---

## 6. Solutions Provided

- **Quality-first sourcing model:** The "We taste before we sell" commitment signals that Macro Frozen evaluates product quality before it reaches customers — providing buyers with confidence that what they order will meet the standard represented.

- **Premium-grade frozen meat and poultry supply:** Macro Frozen positions its product range at the higher end of the frozen protein market, targeting buyers who prioritise quality over lowest-cost commodity supply.

- **Direct B2B wholesale access:** By operating as a wholesale supplier, Macro Frozen gives F&B operators direct access to premium frozen products without the retail mark-up, making quality supply commercially viable for SME buyers.

- **Dedicated B2B sales contact:** A named sales email (sales@macrogroup.biz) and dedicated phone line signal a sales-focused operation structured for business-to-business relationships rather than walk-in retail.
