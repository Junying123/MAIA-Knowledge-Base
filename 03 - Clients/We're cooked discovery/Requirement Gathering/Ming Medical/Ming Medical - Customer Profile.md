## Customer Problems

Hospitals and healthcare institutions in Southeast Asia face a fragmented medical device supply landscape. Key problems Ming Medical's customers carry:

- **Access to specialist devices:** Hospitals cannot source advanced minimally invasive or image-guided devices directly from global OEMs without a local registered distributor — regulatory requirements mandate a licensed importer/distributor for device clearance.
- **Regulatory complexity:** Each medical device must be registered with national health authorities (e.g. HSA in Singapore). Hospitals cannot manage this directly — they rely on distributors to hold and maintain device registrations.
- **Multi-vendor management overhead:** Hospitals would otherwise need to manage dozens of OEM relationships, contracts, pricing, and logistics separately. A single regional distributor simplifies procurement.
- **Specialist product knowledge:** Clinical and procurement teams need technical guidance on device selection, usage, and compatibility. Hospitals lack the OEM-direct relationships to access this support.
- **Supply reliability in SEA:** Global OEMs often lack direct presence in Southeast Asian markets, creating supply chain gaps, long lead times, and limited after-sales support for hospitals.

## Solutions Provided

Ming Medical addresses these problems through:

- **Licensed regional distribution:** Holds device registrations with HSA and equivalent regional authorities, enabling hospitals to legally procure devices without managing compliance themselves.
- **OEM partnership network:** Maintains long-term partnerships with global medical device manufacturers, giving hospitals access to a curated catalogue of specialty devices through a single trusted distributor.
- **Clinical and technical expertise:** 30+ years of domain experience enables Ming Medical to advise procurement teams and clinicians on product selection, clinical applications, and device compatibility.
- **Regional supply chain:** Manages importation, warehousing, and last-mile delivery across Singapore and SEA, reducing lead times and supply disruption risk for healthcare institutions.
- **Regulatory management:** Handles device registration renewals, lot/batch traceability, and compliance documentation — removing a significant administrative burden from hospital procurement teams.
