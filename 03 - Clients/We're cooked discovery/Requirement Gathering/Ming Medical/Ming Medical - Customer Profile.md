Founded in 1999, Ming Medical is a Malaysia-based R&D and commercialisation company specialising in cell-based therapies — specifically Wharton's Jelly Mesenchymal Stem Cells (WJ-MSC), Natural Killer (NK) cells, and exosomes. Recognised as a BioNexus company by the Malaysia Bioeconomy Development Corporation, it operates under the tagline "Giving HOPE where there is none" and positions itself as a provider of cutting-edge, side-effect-free biological treatments for patients with chronic, complex, or treatment-resistant conditions.

## Customer Problems

- **Lack of effective options for complex disease:** Patients with cancers, neurological disorders, or chronic conditions (kidney disease, cardiomyopathy) often exhaust conventional treatment options without full resolution — they need alternative approaches.
- **Side effects from conventional treatments:** Chemotherapy and other standard therapies carry significant side effects; patients and caregivers seek treatments described as more natural and side-effect-free.
- **Antibiotic-resistant infections:** Standard antibiotics fail against resistant bacterial strains, leaving patients without viable treatment options — NK cell therapy is positioned as an alternative.
- **Aging and chronic metabolic disease:** Patients managing diabetes complications, tissue degeneration, or aging-related decline seek therapies that address root causes rather than symptoms.
- **Limited access to cutting-edge therapies:** Regenerative medicine is not widely available in mainstream healthcare — patients must seek out specialist providers.

## Solutions Provided

- **Stem Cell Therapy:** Addresses tissue repair, metabolic, neurological, and orthopedic conditions through biological cell regeneration mechanisms.
- **NK Cell Treatment:** Leverages the immune system's natural killer cells to target cancer cells and drug-resistant pathogens without the toxicity profile of conventional oncology treatments.
- **Exosome Therapy:** Uses nano-scale extracellular vesicles (100–200 nm) derived from MSCs to stimulate healing across multiple organ systems; versatile delivery formats enable targeted administration.
- **Telomere Exosome:** Focuses on cellular longevity and metabolic health, specifically for diabetes management and anti-aging outcomes.
