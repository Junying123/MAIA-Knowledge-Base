# JDX — Discovery Call Questionnaire

> **Read first:** [[JDX]] — GTM brief with company context and initial fit hypothesis.

**Call date:** TBC
**PM:** [Name]
**Attendees:** [Names]

Use this checklist during the call. Check off each item as it's answered. Leave blanks for follow-up.

---

## 1. Sales Channel & Order Intake

*MAIA manages sales orders from creation through to fulfilment. We want to understand how JDX's orders come in today — what channels, what methods, and at what volume — so we can map each flow into MAIA.*

- [ ] Across all your channels, what is the rough % breakdown of orders — consignment (Giant/AEON), salesman (bottle shops), retail stores, online (website/WhatsApp), corporate?
- [ ] For each channel, how exactly does an order come in today? (e.g. WhatsApp message, walk-in, salesman app, website checkout, email PO)
- [ ] Which channel generates the highest order volume per month?
- [ ] Which channel generates the highest revenue?
- [ ] Who handles each type of incoming order — same CS team across all channels, or different people per channel?
- [ ] How many total orders do you process on average per month across all channels?
- [ ] For B2B customers — do they no PO, or just a WhatsApp message / verbal request?
- [ ] Do you issue a quotation before confirming any orders, or go straight to SO?
- [ ] How is each order currently recorded — manually keyed into the ERP, via the salesman app, or auto from Shopify?

---

## 2. Customer Service & Order Processing

*MAIA supports the CS team with order management, real-time stock checks at point of confirmation, and customer communication. We want to understand how JDX handles this today and what systems are involved.*

- [ ] What system does the CS team use today to manage and track orders?
- [ ] When an order comes in, how does CS check whether stock is available before confirming it?
- [ ] Which system holds the live inventory data that CS checks against?
- [ ] What other tools does the team use day-to-day? (ERP, accounting system, spreadsheets, WhatsApp)
- [ ] What happens if an item is out of stock — do you back-order, substitute, or cancel?
- [ ] How do you communicate order status back to the customer (WhatsApp, email, call)?
- [ ] Who is the CS team — how many people and what are their roles?

---

## 3. Pricing

*MAIA supports multiple pricing structures including tier pricing, promotional pricing, region-based pricing, and special customer price lists. We want to understand which of these apply to JDX and how pricing is managed today.*

- [ ] Do you have different price tiers for different customer types (e.g. wholesale vs retail vs corporate)?
- [ ] Do any customers have individually negotiated prices (special rates outside the standard price list)?
- [ ] Do you run promotions or seasonal discounts (e.g. CNY hamper deals, Hari Raya bundles)? How are these applied today — manual or system?
- [ ] Do you price differently by region or by channel (e.g. bottle shop price vs Giant price vs online price)?
- [ ] How is the price list managed and updated today — Excel, ERP, or something else?
- [ ] Do sales agents or store managers have authority to give ad hoc discounts? Is there an approval process?

---

## 4. Finance & Payment

*MAIA manages both credit and cash customer flows — including credit limit controls, payment terms, AR tracking, payment collection, and bank reconciliation. We want to understand how JDX handles each of these today, across all their channels.*

### Credit Customers
- [ ] Which customers are on credit terms (likely Giant, AEON, corporate accounts)?
- [ ] How is a customer's credit limit set — who decides, and on what basis?
- [ ] What happens when a customer hits their credit limit — can it be increased, and who approves?
- [ ] How long before an overdue SO is escalated or cancelled?
- [ ] How do you track outstanding AR today — ERP, Excel, or manually?

### Payment Terms
- [ ] What payment terms do you offer, and does it differ by channel?
  - Cash on spot (salesman channel)?
  - Cash upfront before delivery?
  - Cash within X days after delivery?
  - Credit terms — how many days?
- [ ] Can a single customer have different payment arrangements for different orders?
- [ ] Do customers need to pay before the SO is released to the warehouse for packing?

### Payment Collection & Reconciliation
- [ ] How does a customer notify you of payment — bank slip via WhatsApp, email, or auto-notification?
- [ ] Who receives and records the payment confirmation?
- [ ] How do you match payments to invoices — manually against bank statement, or system-assisted?
- [ ] Who does the bank reconciliation, and how often?
- [ ] Do customers ever partially pay an invoice?

---

## 5. Warehouse & Delivery

*MAIA handles delivery order (DO) generation, delivery scheduling, proof of delivery, and failed delivery management. We want to understand JDX's fulfilment flow — which is more complex given multiple channels (wholesale, retail stores, online, corporate) drawing from the same warehouse.*

### Warehouse Operations
- [ ] How does the warehouse team know which orders to pick and pack — printed SO/DO, WhatsApp, or system notification?
- [ ] Who generates the DO, and at what point in the process?
- [ ] Is picking and packing done manually or is there any system guiding it?

### Delivery Modes
- [ ] What % of orders are self-collect vs managed delivery, across all channels?
- [ ] For deliveries you manage — do you use your own drivers, third-party couriers (e.g. PosLaju, J&T, DHL), or both?
- [ ] Which channels use which delivery method?

### Delivery Execution
- [ ] How do you currently schedule and assign deliveries — manually, WhatsApp to driver, or system?
- [ ] What happens when a delivery fails (customer unavailable) — how is it rescheduled?
- [ ] When a customer is not available, does the driver take a photo as proof — is this currently done or something you want?
- [ ] Does the customer sign a physical DO on delivery, or is proof of delivery done digitally?

---

## 6. Returns & Credit Notes

*MAIA supports the full return workflow — from customer return request through to credit note issuance, inventory adjustment, and disposal. We want to understand what return scenarios JDX deals with and how credit notes are currently handled.*

### Returns
- [ ] What is your approximate return rate, and what are the most common reasons?
- [ ] Walk us through a return — which channel does it happen most, and what's the step-by-step process?
- [ ] How do you assess whether a returned item is damaged or not?
  - If not damaged → product is exchanged. Is this a same-day swap or a new order?
  - If damaged → inventory is adjusted and product disposed. Who approves?
- [ ] For consignment (Giant/AEON) — do they ever return unsold stock back to you, especially after CNY/Hari Raya?
- [ ] Do any products have shelf-life or expiry considerations that affect return eligibility (e.g. marine delicacies, wellness products)?

### Credit Notes
- [ ] What types of credit notes do you issue today — for returns, pricing adjustments, damaged goods, others?
- [ ] Are there different credit note types depending on the scenario (e.g. regular product return vs a custom/assembled hamper return)?
- [ ] How are credit notes currently created — in the ERP, manually, or elsewhere?
- [ ] Can a credit note be offset against a future invoice, or is it always settled as a cash refund?

---

## 7. Inventory & Forecasting

*JDX-specific — flagged in GTM brief as a major pain point. Critical to understand given multiple channels and 6+ locations all drawing from shared stock.*

- [ ] Where is inventory currently tracked — single system, or split across SQL ERP, salesman app, store POS, and Shopify separately?
- [ ] Is there a single inventory pool, or is stock allocated separately per channel / per location?
- [ ] How do you decide how much stock to place at each outlet (Giant, AEON, bottle shops, each store)?
- [ ] Is forecasting truly by gut feel, or is there an Excel / data file behind it?
- [ ] How far in advance do you plan stock allocation — weekly, monthly?
- [ ] How do you handle seasonal spikes (CNY, Hari Raya)? Is there a documented process or is it ad hoc?
- [ ] Do you track sell-through per outlet individually, or just total sales?
- [ ] What triggers a replenishment — a fixed schedule, or when stock hits a threshold?
- [ ] Have you ever run out of stock at an outlet during a peak season? How was it handled?

---

## 8. Consignment Workflow

*JDX-specific — non-standard channel, flagged as a likely customisation need. No PO involved — JDX controls placement, invoices on sell-through.*

- [ ] Walk us through the full consignment cycle with Giant/AEON end-to-end:
  - How do you decide quantity and product mix to place at each outlet?
  - How do you track what's been placed vs what's been sold?
  - How often do you reconcile sell-through?
  - What triggers the invoice — placement, sell-through report received, or a fixed periodic schedule?
- [ ] Who physically restocks the shelf at each outlet — your team or theirs?
- [ ] Do Giant/AEON send you a sell-through report (format, frequency), or does your team go in to check manually?
- [ ] For salesman direct sales at bottle shops:
  - The salesman visits, takes the order, and issues invoice on the spot — is payment also collected immediately?
  - What app does the salesman use today? What does it do well and what's missing?
  - Does the salesman app sync back to your main ERP, or is it completely disconnected?
- [ ] Are there any other consignment customers beyond Giant and AEON?

---

## 9. Current Systems & Integrations

*MAIA will need to integrate with or replace existing tools. We want a full map of every system in use today and where the data gaps are.*

- [ ] Give us a full list of every system your team uses today — ERP, accounting, salesman app, store POS, e-commerce, spreadsheets
- [ ] Which system is the single source of truth for inventory?
- [ ] Which system is the source of truth for finance and AR?
- [ ] Are any of these systems currently integrated, or is data moved manually between them?
- [ ] Where are the biggest data gaps or manual steps today?
- [ ] For the salesman app — would you want MAIA to replace it, or integrate with it?
- [ ] For Shopify — would you want MAIA to sync inventory and orders from the website, or keep online separate?
- [ ] For the store POS (if any) — is this in scope to connect to MAIA, or manage separately?
- [ ] What accounting system are you on — SQL Accounting, Autocount, Xero? Is the finance team open to moving?
