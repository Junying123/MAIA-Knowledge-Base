# PDF Output Review

## Overview

This folder tracks the review of PDF documents generated by the MAIA system. The goal is to verify that all system-generated PDFs (invoices, quotations, sales orders, credit notes, etc.) are accurate, correctly formatted, and meet client expectations before go-live or after system updates.

## Review Checklist (per document)

- [ ] Header/logo displays correctly
- [ ] Company and client details are accurate
- [ ] Line items, quantities, and pricing are correct
- [ ] Totals and taxes calculate correctly
- [ ] Document number and date format correct
- [ ] Footer and terms display correctly
- [ ] PDF renders cleanly (no broken layout)
