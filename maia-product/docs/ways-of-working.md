# Ways Of Working

## Purpose
This document describes how the MAIA teams typically work together across product, tech, and GTM.

## Main Working Groups
- Product team
- Tech team
- GTM / Sales / Marketing team

See `docs/mindhive-company-context.md` for team members and leadership context.

## Working Principles
- Keep important context in shared docs, not only in chat or in one person's head.
- Keep formal implementation specs in MAIA CODEX.
- Keep live status, official ownership, dates, and published docs in Lark / Mindhive OS.
- Keep this repo as the working context layer for product operations and onboarding.

## Typical Product Flow
1. A request, opportunity, issue, or client need is identified.
2. Product context is gathered from existing docs, Lark, meetings, and prior work.
3. Scope is clarified and documented.
4. When the work is implementation-ready, the formal spec or handoff is created in the appropriate source-of-truth location.
5. Product and tech align on delivery.
6. Delivery progress is tracked in the live operating systems, not invented locally.

## Product Team Responsibilities
- clarify problem and scope
- maintain product context
- support handoff quality
- make sure important decisions are documented

## Tech Team Responsibilities
- review implementation feasibility
- execute frontend, backend, chatbot, integration, and infrastructure work
- surface constraints, dependencies, and technical risks

## GTM / Sales / Marketing Responsibilities
- provide market, sales, customer, and go-to-market context
- align messaging and commercial context where needed
- help close the loop between product direction and market-facing needs

## Handoff Expectations
A good handoff should make clear:
- what problem is being solved
- who the users or stakeholders are
- what the current scope is
- what assumptions or risks exist
- where the source materials came from

Use existing templates and checklists in:
- `templates/`
- `agent-ops/checklists/`
- `agent-ops/runbooks/`

## Documentation Expectations
Update shared documentation when:
- responsibilities change
- a meaningful decision is made
- a repeatable process changes
- a new recurring term or workflow appears

## What Not To Do
- do not treat memory as personal only
- do not leave important decisions undocumented
- do not duplicate live trackers locally without purpose
- do not treat temporary chat context as durable context
