# Drop Ship (ERPNext Selling Module)

Ship directly from supplier to customer

## MAIA Relevance
For MAIA's WhatsApp-first AI order-to-cash platform:
- **WhatsApp Integration**: Potential for receiving quotations/orders via WhatsApp
- **AI Enhancement**: Could use AI to suggest quotation items based on context
- **Automation**: AI could automate follow-ups on quotations/orders
- **Context Learning**: Sales interactions stored in ERPNext provide learning data
