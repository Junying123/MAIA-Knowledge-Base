# ERPNext

ERPNext is business management software that helps companies stay on top of their operations, whether it's tracking inventory, managing finances, handling projects, or keeping customers happy. Think of it as the central nervous system for your business, bringing everything together in one place.

## Why Choose ERPNext?
- **Complete suite**: Built-in tools for all business needs (Assets to Accounting to Inventory)
- **No vendor lock-in**: Open-source means you own your data and have freedom to customize
- **User-friendly**: Clean, modern interface (not built in the '90s)
- **Cost-effective**: No sky-high licensing fees

## Key Capabilities
ERPNext helps organizations:
1. Track financial indicators
2. Manage invoices and payments
3. Monitor inventory levels
4. Monitor KPIs
5. Handle customer queries
6. Process payroll
7. Manage tasks
8. Maintain customer/supplier/contact database
9. Prepare quotations
10. Track budgets
11. Calculate cost-based pricing
12. Schedule maintenance reminders
13. Publish websites

## Technical Foundation
- **Frappe Framework**: Full-stack web application framework (Python/Javascript) providing database abstraction, authentication, REST API
- **Frappe UI**: Vue-based UI library for modern interfaces
