# ERPNext Selling Module

## Overview
The Selling module in ERPNext manages the entire sales process from lead generation to delivery and billing. It includes tools for quotations, sales orders, deliveries, and invoices.

## Key Features Visible in Navigation:
From the documentation structure observed:

### Core Transactions:
- **Quotation**: Create and manage sales quotations
- **Sales Order**: Process confirmed sales orders from quotations
- **Blanket Order**: Framework orders for repeated deliveries
- **Drop Ship**: Ship directly from supplier to customer
- **Sales Return**: Handle returned goods from customers
- **Sales Partner**: Manage channel partners and commissions
- **Sales Person**: Track individual salesperson performance
- **Credit Limit**: Set and monitor customer credit limits
- **Calculate Incentive For Sales Team**: Commission and bonus calculations
- **Promotional Scheme**: Manage discounts and promotional offers
- **Sales Cycle Integration**: Integrate sales process with other modules
- **Inter Company Invoices**: Handle billing between company subsidiaries
